How Lantern answers

Three steps, all running in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

  1. Search by meaningEvery passage of your documents is turned into 384 numbers that capture its meaning (BGE-small). Your question too. The closest passages win, so "my father passed away" finds "bereavement leave", with no shared words.
  2. Read sentence by sentenceA second model (bge-reranker-base) reads your question together with each sentence of the 4 best passages, and scores how well it answers.
  3. Point, or admit itThe best sentence is shown and lit up on its page. If its score is too low, Lantern says it couldn't find the answer, instead of making one up.
